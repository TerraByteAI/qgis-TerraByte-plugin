about=Road Extractor draws seven line types (highway, local road, path, railway, waterway, runway and power line) from an aerial or satellite raster you already have in QGIS. A TerraByte account is required: sign in with it, pick a raster, draw a rectangle and run the model. The imagery inside the rectangle is uploaded to TerraByte's cloud service to run the model and is not kept; the lines come back to the map. Keep the line types you want, delete the lines you do not, and export a GeoPackage, GeoJSON or Shapefile layer. Each run uses tiles from your monthly allowance, and the run check shows the tiles before you start. Needs an internet connection.
